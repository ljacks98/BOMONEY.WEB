The Story

About BOMONEY

BOMONEY is unorthodox, uncut, and unlike no other. He believes in doing his music, his way.

He appreciates every artistic ability God has blessed him with, and although he is stepping out late to his calling, he knows it was supposed to be that way. Every one of his life experiences, from joys to pains, has been the catalyst for the intricate lyrics you hear in his music.

Born in the Lower Ninth Ward of New Orleans, Louisiana, he is the middle child of three boys. True to his name's origin, "strong and wise warrior," BOMONEY has proven to be just that.

An independent hip-hop and rap artist, producer, and creator, he is known for his 2026 album Better Days Ahead and singles like "Trap Phone Freestyle" and "QUEST." His sound focuses on self-produced beats, independent studio flow, and raw underground hip-hop.
